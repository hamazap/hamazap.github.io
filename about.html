ABOUT THIS LITTLE CORNER OF THE INTERNET MADE BY GIRLS FOR GIRLS

Dear girl, this is for you.

DearDiary is a digital scrapbook dedicated to the experiences, ideas, memories, and questions that shape what it means to grow up as a girl.

Through fashion, personal essays, interviews, history, and conversations, this publication explores the things we wear, the emotions we feel, the people we become, the media that influences us, and the stories we don't always get to tell.

This isn't about defining girlhood for everyone. Every girl has a different story, and that's exactly why.

Think of this space as your diary and an archive: a place to collect little moments, ask bigger questions, learn something new, and recognize pieces of yourself in someone else's story.
